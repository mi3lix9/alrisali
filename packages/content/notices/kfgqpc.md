# King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex (KFGQPC)

The Quran text, the page and line of every ayah on the 604 fifteen-line Madinah pages, and the KFGQPC HAFS Uthmanic Script font come from KFGQPC's developer release for Hafs, `kfgqpc_hafs_v30` (https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/quran-dev/, zip SHA-256 227e6b1564d980f2bd09c2c35ebfb0330ac268c79a7c247cd1ab665bc635f245).

- The text is reproduced verbatim. Where an ayah that spans several lines breaks was worked out from the mushaf PDF in the same release.
- The font is bundled unmodified, under the licence embedded in it (quoted verbatim below).
