# Adhan recordings — the default voices of the Adhan catalogue

Published by `bun run adhans:seed` (apps/server/seed/adhans). Each voice has its opening takbeer (a WAV cut under 30 seconds, so iOS can play it as a notification sound) and its full adhan (AAC, `.m4a`).

## Saeed Hatamzadeh-Varmazyar

Source: “AzaanMaahur.ogg” (اذان، در دستگاه ماهور), Wikimedia Commons, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AzaanMaahur.ogg (SHA-1 2f6cd3bc6110a77c4f21abad33f4c0986467aebc). The Shia (Ja'fari) Adhan, used as such in the English and Persian Wikipedia “Adhan” articles. Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. Not endorsed by the author.

Changes: the takbeer is the opening 28.8 seconds (3.5 s to 32.3 s of the original, ending on a pause) with a 0.2 s fade-in and a 1.5 s fade-out, as 22.05 kHz mono 16-bit WAV; the full adhan is the whole recording converted from Ogg Vorbis to 48 kbit/s AAC. The adapted files are shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.
