## Changes

Cut to its opening 28.8 seconds (3.5 s to 32.3 s of the original: the opening takbirs, ending on a pause), with a 0.2 s fade-in and a 1.5 s fade-out, and converted from Ogg Vorbis to 22.05 kHz mono 16-bit WAV (`apps/native/assets/sounds/adhan.wav`), so iOS (which plays at most 30 seconds of a notification sound) and Android can play it as a notification sound. The adapted file is shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.
